The Letter I Wrote For You

Happy 1st Anniversary, Bebi! ❤️

Happy first anniversary, Bebi! One year na tayo, and honestly, I'm just really happy that I get to share this journey with you. Thank you for all the happiness, the laughs, the lambing, and even the simplest moments we've shared. I hope you know how much I appreciate having you in my life.

I know hindi naman laging smooth ang relationship natin. May misunderstandings, tampuhan, and times na hindi tayo nagkakaintindihan, but I'm happy that we're still here, trying to understand each other and making things work. I don't expect us to be perfect. I just want us to keep choosing each other, even when things get difficult.

Babe, I'm really proud of you. I see your efforts, and I know you have your own problems and struggles na minsan hindi mo rin sinasabi. Please don't let those things bring you down. Don't be too hard on yourself, okay? I believe in you, and I hope you never forget that you have someone who will always support you and be proud of you, even in your smallest achievements.

I also want you to feel assured that I'm serious about us. I'm not here just for the happy days or for the easy parts of our relationship. I want to stay loyal, be honest with you, and make you feel loved and secure. I know I have my own shortcomings, but I'll always try to be a better partner for you. I want you to feel that you can trust me and that you never have to question where you stand in my life.

And of course, I still want more with you. More dates, more gala, more food trips, more random pictures, more memories, and more anniversaries to celebrate. Marami pa tayong gustong gawin and places na gustong puntahan, and I hope we get to experience all of those together.

Thank you for this first year, Bebi. I'm happy that it's you, and if I had to choose again, I'd still choose you. I know we still have a lot to learn about each other, but I hope we never get tired of trying.

One year down, and more years to go. Happy anniversary, Babe! I love you so much. ❤️

Our Milestones

The Chapters of Our First Year

Chapter 1

The Day We Met

What started as a simple meet-up at MOA became the beginning of everything. It was already getting late when we met, and we ended up spending hours by the seaside just talking and getting to know each other. The cool breeze, the view, and our conversations made the night feel easy and comfortable.

Later, we shared a meal at Marugame, laughing over random stories and enjoying each other's company. Looking back now, it was just a normal day on the calendar, but somehow it became one of the most important days of my life.

Chapter 2

Our First Date

One of my favorite memories from the beginning of our story was our first date at Ayala Malls Manila Bay. The weather was rainy around that time, and we both showed up in simple outfits without trying too hard to impress each other.

We spent most of our time at the rooftop, talking about anything and everything. There was nothing extravagant about the day, but that's exactly what made it special. It felt comfortable, natural, and effortless, like we could spend hours together and still have more things to talk about.

Chapter 3

The Unexpected Beginning

Not every love story starts with a grand gesture, and ours certainly didn't. We were just spending a random day together at Arroceros, enjoying the moment and walking around without any expectations.

Then, out of nowhere, everything changed. You finally said yes. As if the moment wasn't already unforgettable, it even started drizzling shortly afterward. It wasn't planned, dramatic, or perfect, but it became one of the most beautiful moments of our story.

Chapter 4

Our First Staycation

Our trip to Tagaytay will always be one of my favorite memories from our first year together. It was our first staycation, and for a little while, it felt like we were already living together.

We did the simplest things together, grocery shopping, cooking meals, relaxing, and enjoying each other's company. What made it special wasn't the destination itself, but the feeling of sharing everyday moments side by side.

One memory that still makes me smile is when we went out late at night just to grab coffee. Walking back through the dark streets was both funny and slightly scary, but those little moments ended up becoming some of my favorite memories from the trip.

Chapter 5

Our Samgyup Days

If there's one thing that has become part of our relationship, it's our endless samgyupsal cravings. Somehow every samgyup date turns into hours of eating, laughing, telling stories, and making each other laugh until our stomachs hurt.

After every meal, we always say the same thing: that we'll take a break from samgyupsal for a while. But somehow, after just a few days, we're already talking about where we're going to eat next. It's become one of our favorite traditions, and honestly, I wouldn't want it any other way.

Chapter 6

The Best Surprise Came With Four Paws

One of my favorite monthsary memories happened completely by accident. I originally wanted to give you flowers, but after searching around, I couldn't find any. Instead of giving up, I ended up bringing home something much more unexpected.

That day, Bella came into our lives.

What started as a last-minute alternative turned into one of the most meaningful surprises we've shared. Bella became our first dog, and from that moment on, she became part of our little family. Looking back, I honestly think bringing Bella home was better than any bouquet I could have bought.

She gave us new memories, new responsibilities, and so many moments that made us smile. And every time I see her, I'm reminded of that random monthsary when a simple plan turned into something we'll never forget.

Chapter 7

Our Museum Adventure

One of the most unforgettable experiences we shared was our museum date. What made it even more special was that it was the first time for both of us.

We walked through the exhibits together, taking our time to look at everything and learning new things along the way. There were so many moments where we just stood there amazed by what we were seeing. Every room seemed to offer something different, and experiencing it together made it even more memorable.

What I loved most wasn't just the museum itself, but seeing your reactions, hearing your thoughts, and sharing that sense of curiosity together. It was one of those dates where the experience felt completely new for both of us, and somehow that made it even more special.

By the end of the day, we left with more than just photos. We left with another memory that became part of our story, another adventure that we'll always look back on and smile about.
